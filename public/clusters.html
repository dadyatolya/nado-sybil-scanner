Unofficial community tool — not affiliated with or endorsed by Nado. Cluster flags are statistical heuristics, not proof of wrongdoing. Verify anything important yourself.

Live scan of recent protocol activity for two Sybil patterns: wallets funded with near-identical amounts minutes apart, and wallets that mirror each other's long/short trades. Scans are windowed and bounded — widen the window for a deeper (slower) scan.

① Funding pattern — same-amount deposits, ≤5% off, within 10 minutes

② Mirror trading — opposite long/short, ≤5% size diff, within 15 seconds, open + close, ≥10 matched trades

Run a scan to see results.

Funding-pattern data: Ink Explorer (on-chain deposits to Nado's Clearinghouse contract). Mirror-trading data: Nado Archive API (off-chain trade tape — individual trades never touch the chain, so the block explorer alone can't see them).
